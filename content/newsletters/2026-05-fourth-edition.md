# Letter from our Founder

May is officially here, and we have a lot to celebrate at BLPAPA! Let's catch
up on the big updates:

## Celebrating AAPI Heritage Month

This month, we honor the rich history, diverse cultures, and incredible
contributions of the Asian American and Pacific Islander communities. Within
our own walls, we are so grateful for our AAPI artists and volunteers who bring
vital perspectives and talent to our mission. We encourage everyone to take a
moment this month to engage with AAPI stories—whether through film, literature,
or local community events.

## Happy BDOG!

It's that time of year! Today we celebrate Big Day of Giving (BDOG). This is a
massive moment for us to secure the resources we need to keep our programs
thriving. If you're able to share our donation link or post on social media,
every bit of noise helps us hit our goal. Let's show everyone the power of the
BLPAPA community! BDOG if May 7, you can start donating now:
<https://www.bigdayofgiving.org/organization/Bukang-Liwayway-Pilipino-Amerikano-Pangarap-Asosasyon>

## We Have a New Home!

The rumors are true: we've officially secured a new rehearsal and community
space! Having a dedicated spot to create, practice, and collaborate is a total
game-changer. We look forward to welcoming you all soon whether for learning
new dances, or coming together socially.

Thank you for being the heart of everything we do. Let's make this May one for
the books!

With Gratitude,
**Dr. Edith Montemayor**
Founder, CEO, and President
