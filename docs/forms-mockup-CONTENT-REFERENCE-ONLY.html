About us

Culture, identity, community, and pride.

Bukang Liwayway Pilipino-Amerikano Pangarap Asosasyon (BLPAPA) is a nonprofit organization founded in 2023 under the direction of Dr. Edith Montemayor. We are dedicated to preserving and promoting the rich cultural heritage of the Philippines within the Filipino-American community. Our mission is to foster a strong sense of identity, community, and pride through cultural education, artistic expression, and community engagement. We envision a vibrant community where Filipino-American culture thrives and is celebrated by all.

Our organization celebrates the growing Filipino community, the second-largest Asian group in Elk Grove, where we operate and are headquartered. Our rapid growth demonstrates our increasing importance and appeal to the community. Our events, membership, and following foster cultural pride, unity, and understanding while enriching the fabric of our diverse city.

Our classes and events give local Filipino dancers, businesses, and artists a platform to share their talents, contribute to a thriving arts and culture scene, promote AAPI businesses, and build connections. Attendees come from the greater Sacramento region, the Bay Area, and Stockton, strengthening Filipino community, solidarity, and unity.

About FAHM & Bayanihan

A celebration rooted in history.

Filipino American History Month (FAHM) is celebrated annually in October to commemorate the arrival of the first Filipinos in the continental United States on October 18, 1587, in Morro Bay, California. It is a time to celebrate the rich history, culture, and contributions of Filipino Americans to the United States.

The 4th Annual Bayanihan Festival takes place just ahead of FAHM on Sunday, September 27, 2026 at District 56 in the heart of Elk Grove. The festival highlights Philippine traditions through local artists, traditional folk dances, music, Filipino-owned businesses, and youth engagement. It strengthens young people's connection to Filipino heritage, combats the erosion of cultural identity, and creates a joyous space for pride, unity, and understanding.
