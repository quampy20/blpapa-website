In their words

What is Bayanihan Festival?

BLPAPA's Bayanihan Festival is a vibrant celebration dedicated to honoring and showcasing Filipino and Filipino-American culture. The festival highlights the rich traditions of the Philippines through a diverse array of activities, including performances by local artists, traditional Philippine folk dances and music, and sometimes, appearances by well-known, larger Filipino artists. It also supports and promotes Filipino-owned businesses, creating a platform for them to connect with the community. A key focus of the festival is engaging the youth, aiming to strengthen their connection to Filipino heritage and combat the erosion of cultural identity. Overall, the Bayanihan Festival is a joyous occasion that fosters cultural pride, unity, and understanding.

How it started

Bayanihan Festival's History

BLPAPA's Bayanihan Festival kicked off its first event on October 8, 2023, turning a dream of CEO Edith Montemayor into reality. Planning started back in June 2023, and the festival quickly came together with exciting performances by Jules Aurora and Jamieboy. This debut was a huge hit, attracting over 5,000 people who enjoyed the performances of local artists and explored offerings from 70+ vendors. This landmark occasion set the stage for future celebrations, embodying a deep commitment to cultural pride and community connection.

What the day is like

A whole day of it, and none of it costs anything to walk into

On the stage

Traditional folk dance, live music, and local performers. Our own dancers perform alongside invited artists. Our inaugural year brought Jules Aurora and Jamieboy to the stage.

Street eats

Filipinos speak in the language of food. The food truck and food tent section is where a good part of the day happens.

Karaoke contest

There is a karaoke contest. There was never any question that there would be a karaoke contest.

Five ways to be in it, not just at it

Each one goes to a different team and asks for different things. Find the one that describes you.

Volunteer

For you if you can give a few hours on the day. Setup from 7 AM, the festival itself from 11 to 4, and teardown after. Roles include helping vendors and performers, the kids' zone, setup, and teardown. Students looking for community service hours are welcome, and so are corporate volunteer groups.

Apply by 6 September
Volunteer sign-up

Perform on the stage

For you if you are a musician, dancer, or other performer, solo or in a group, and you want a set at the festival. Sets run about 20 to 25 minutes. There is no compensation for performances, and because stage time is limited, acceptance is not guaranteed.

Apply by 23 August
Performer application

Dance with BLPAPA

For you if you want to learn Filipino folk dance and perform it with our own group rather than bringing your own act. Rehearsals run Fridays through the season in Elk Grove, and your first class is free if you are new to us. No experience needed.

Dancer application

Vend at the festival

For you if you sell food, retail, or want an informational booth. Indoor and outdoor spaces are both in use this year. Food vendors need their own health permits, and every booth is inspected by the Elk Grove Fire Department.

Required of every vendor

  • Outdoor canopies need weights on every leg and CPAI-83 certification.
  • Food vendors obtain their own health permits and documentation. Proof is requested after acceptance.
  • Booths are inspected by the Elk Grove Fire Department; food booths may also be inspected by a public health inspector.
  • Alcohol is prohibited.
