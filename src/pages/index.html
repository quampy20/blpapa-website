Our mission in action

Celebrate Filipino culture. Connect generations. Carry the legacy forward.

BLPAPA is the sole Filipino-American organization in Elk Grove dedicated to preserving and celebrating Filipino culture. Founded in 2023, we do that in the most ordinary and most powerful way there is: together, in a room, in person.

Celebrate

Vibrant events where the music is loud, the food is real, and nobody has to explain themselves. The Bayanihan Festival is the biggest, and it is free to attend.

See what is coming up

Connect

Lolas and lolos beside teenagers, new arrivals beside third generation kababayan. Intergenerational connection is written into our vision, and it is why we meet face to face.

Carry forward

Dance, song, language, and cooking passed hand to hand to the next generation. What gets taught gets kept.

Programs

Learning that happens in a room together

Our flagship event

The Bayanihan Festival

Bayanihan is the old picture of neighbours lifting a whole house together and carrying it down the road. That is the day: local artists, folk dance, Filipino-owned businesses, food, and several thousand neighbours in one place.
