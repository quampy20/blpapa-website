About us

Culture, identity, community, and pride

Bukang Liwayway means dawn. We took the name because that is what this work feels like: something old coming back into the light, and something new starting.

Our mission

Our organization is dedicated to promoting and preserving the rich heritage of Filipino culture. Through vibrant events, educational programs, and community engagement, we aim to celebrate and share the traditions, values, and artistic expressions that define our identity.

Our vision

A future where the Filipino culture is celebrated, cherished, and integrated into the fabric of society. We envision a vibrant community that embraces our rich heritage, fosters intergenerational connections.

Our story

Founded in 2023, in Elk Grove

Bukang Liwayway Pilipino-Amerikano Pangarap Asosasyon (BLPAPA) is a non-profit organization founded in 2023, under the direction of Dr. Edith Montemayor. We are dedicated to preserving and promoting the rich cultural heritage of the Philippines within the Filipino-American community. Our mission is to foster a strong sense of identity, community, and pride through cultural education, artistic expression, and community engagement. We envision a vibrant community where Filipino-American culture thrives and is celebrated by all.

Our organization celebrates the growing Filipino community, the second-largest Asian group in Elk Grove where we operate and are headquartered. Our rapid growth, demonstrates an increasing importance and appeal to our community. Our events, membership, and following fosters cultural pride, unity, and understanding, enriching the fabric of our city's diverse community. Our classes and events have a positive impact on the region in several ways. It provides a platform for local Filipino dancers, businesses, and artists to showcase their talents, and contribute to thriving arts and culture scene as well as promote AAPI businesses through networking. Our classes and events attract attendees from all over the greater Sacramento region, the Bay Area and Stockton, bringing together more Filipino community, solidarity and unity.

In May 2026 we secured a dedicated rehearsal and community space of our own. Somewhere to practice, create, and gather changes what an organization our age can do.

Filipino American History Month

A celebration rooted in history

Filipino American History Month (FAHM) is celebrated annually in October to commemorate the arrival of the first Filipinos in the continental United States on October 18, 1587, in Morro Bay, California. It's a time to celebrate the rich history, culture, and contributions of Filipino Americans to the United States.

Our event, the 4th Annual Bayanihan Festival will take place just ahead of FAHM on Sunday, September 27, 2026 at District 56 in the heart of Elk Grove. The festival highlights the rich traditions of the Philippines through a diverse array of activities, including performances by local artists, traditional Philippine folk dances and music. It also supports and promotes Filipino-owned businesses, creating a platform for them to connect with the community. A key focus of the festival is engaging the youth, aiming to strengthen their connection to Filipino heritage and combat the erosion of cultural identity. Overall, the Bayanihan Festival is a joyous occasion that fosters cultural pride, unity, and understanding.
