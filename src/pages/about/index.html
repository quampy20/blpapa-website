About us

Culture, identity, community, and pride

Bukang Liwayway means dawn. We took the name because that is what this work feels like: something old coming back into the light, and something new starting.

Our mission

Our organization is dedicated to promoting and preserving the rich heritage of Filipino culture. Through vibrant events, educational programs, and community engagement, we aim to celebrate and share the traditions, values, and artistic expressions that define our identity.

Our vision

A future where the Filipino culture is celebrated, cherished, and integrated into the fabric of society. We envision a vibrant community that embraces our rich heritage, fosters intergenerational connections.

Our story

Founded in 2023, in Elk Grove

BLPAPA was founded in 2023 under the direction of Dr. Edith Montemayor. We are the sole Filipino-American organization in Elk Grove dedicated to preserving and celebrating Filipino culture.

Filipinos are the second-largest Asian community in Elk Grove, and the city is where we operate and are headquartered. Our classes and events give local Filipino dancers, businesses, and artists a platform to share their talents, contribute to a thriving arts and culture scene, promote AAPI businesses, and build connections. Attendees come from the greater Sacramento region, the Bay Area, and Stockton.

In May 2026 we secured a dedicated rehearsal and community space of our own. Somewhere to practice, create, and gather changes what an organization our age can do.

Filipino American History Month

A celebration rooted in history

Filipino American History Month is celebrated every October, marking the arrival of the first Filipinos in the continental United States on 18 October 1587, at Morro Bay, California. It is a month for the history, the culture, and the contributions of Filipino Americans.

The Bayanihan Festival lands just ahead of it. The festival highlights Philippine traditions through local artists, traditional folk dances, music, Filipino-owned businesses, and youth engagement. It strengthens young people's connection to Filipino heritage, works against the erosion of cultural identity, and makes a joyful space for pride, unity, and understanding.
