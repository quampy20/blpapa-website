Support our mission

Keep the festival free and the dancing going

BLPAPA is a young organization doing something nobody else in Elk Grove is doing. Giving is how the doors stay open and the gate stays free.

What your gift supports

We are deliberately not going to tell you that a particular amount buys a particular thing. We would have to make that number up, and this is not the place for made-up numbers. Here is what giving actually goes towards.

  • A festival that costs nothing to walk into. Free admission is a choice. It is paid for by vendors, sponsors, and donors so that cost is never why a family stays home.
  • The Performing Arts Program. Rehearsal space, teaching, and everything it takes to get a group of dancers from a first class to a stage.
  • The Educational Program we are building. Tagalog lessons, Filipino cuisine instruction, and native instrument classes, planned as we grow.
  • A room of our own. As of May 2026 we have a dedicated rehearsal and community space, which changes what an organization our size can offer.
