Set timing and playback

Set timing. Each set is roughly 20 to 25 minutes, with 10 minutes to prepare before and after. If your setup or takedown is brief, your performance time may extend within the assigned range.

Playback. Performers using playback or tracks must use USB, CD, or MP3 files. After acceptance, email your audio to the confirmation address, and bring any USB adapter or AUX connection you need on the day.
